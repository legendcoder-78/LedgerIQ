How LedgerIQ Works

1
Ingest & Normalize:

Ingests multi-source data feeds (Bank, Gateway, Invoices) and cleans messy merchant names via local Ollama LLM.

2
Reconcile (3-Tier Cascade):

Executes Exact SQL → Subset-Sum (1 Lump = N Invoices minus fee) → RapidFuzz Semantic Matching.

3
Detect & Resolve Exceptions:

Triage risk (HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW), drafts double-entry accounting adjustments via LLM diagnostic agent, and learns adaptive rules upon human approval.

4
Forecast Cash & Stress Test:

Projects 30-day runway using settled cash + risk-adjusted receivables, and simulates collection delays or fee surges.

5
Immutable Audit Trail:

Every automated match, diagnosis, and human resolution is permanently written to an append-only audit log for regulatory compliance.
